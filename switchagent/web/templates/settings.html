🧪 Turn on experimental features?

These features are new and still being tested. They may change, behave unexpectedly, or be removed.

The Add-ons tab installs other people's open-source homebrew onto your Switch — system modules and overlays among it. Some need a restart of the console to start, and a module that does not suit your firmware can keep it from booting until it is removed from the SD card by hand.

You can turn them off here at any time; what is already on the Switch stays there.

Appearance

Library cards and the running blocks on progress bars. Auto follows your system's reduced-motion preference, which Remote Desktop often turns on by itself; On animates regardless. Saved automatically in this browser.

Library automation

Covers: TitleDB (US English), images from Nintendo. Cached locally; unavailable covers never block scanning or installation. New files are indexed automatically; installation still requires your selection.

"On Switch" highlight in the Library

Enable "Installed Games" in DBI's own settings on the console (in DBI: Settings → the option that shows installed titles as an "Installed games" MTP entry — sometimes labelled "Show Combined NSP in Installed Games"). Once it's on, SwitchAgent reads that list over MTP and highlights any matching game in your Library green, with an "On Switch" badge — a live report from DBI itself, not just SwitchAgent's own transfer history.

Matching is by game name (DBI's listing has no TITLE_ID), so it's best-effort — case, punctuation and trademark symbols are ignored, but a very different display name won't match. If the option is missing or the list stays empty after enabling it, your DBI build/console may not expose this MTP node at all.
